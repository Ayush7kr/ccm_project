Platform Capabilities

Built for Complete Tele-calling Workflows

From campaign inception to final reporting, CCM bridges the gap between management oversight and tele-caller operational efficiency.

Campaign Management

Create, configure, and monitor multi-stage calling campaigns with defined targets, date schedules, and progress tracking.

  • Lifecycle statuses: Draft, Active, Paused, Completed
  • Dynamic target progress calculations
  • Real-time completion percentage tracking

Customer & Lead Management

Centralized directory of contacts with automated duplicate detection, bulk CSV/XLSX import, and flexible tele-caller assignment.

  • Validated CSV and XLSX spreadsheet importer
  • Round-robin and manual lead assignment
  • Complete interaction and call history logs

Live Calling Console

Dedicated distraction-free interface for tele-callers to dial customers, track call duration, and capture questionnaire data in real time.

  • Standardized outcomes: Completed, Busy, No Answer
  • Live stopwatch timer for accurate durations
  • Integrated follow-up scheduler during calls

Questionnaire Builder

Attach structured survey questionnaires directly to campaigns with multiple question formats to ensure standardized customer data collection.

  • Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Yes/No
  • 1–5 Rating Scales & Open-Ended suggestions
  • Interactive questionnaire preview tool

Follow-up Tracking

Never miss an interested prospect. Schedule callbacks with specific dates and times, with automated overdue detection and priority queues.

  • Daily callback queue for tele-callers
  • Automated overdue status management
  • Admin oversight of pending follow-up workload

Analytics & Report Center

Enterprise management dashboard with date range filters, call outcome donuts, agent productivity metrics, and PDF/Excel downloads.

  • Publication-grade PDF exports via ReportLab
  • Formatted Excel exports via OpenPyXL
  • 100% database-derived unified metrics engine

1

Access Architecture

Strict Role-Based Access Control

CCM enforces clear operational separation between supervisory management and tele-calling execution.

Management Role

System Administrator (ADMIN)

Full oversight of organization campaigns, customer databases, tele-caller workforce, and executive reports.

  • Campaign Control: Full CRUD operations, status transitions, and target definition.
  • Data Operations: Bulk CSV/XLSX customer import and assignment to agents.
  • Advanced Analytics: Global date-filtered performance metrics, outcomes, and agent comparisons.
  • Report Center: Previews and formal downloads of PDF and Excel operational reports.
  • Team Management: Provisioning and monitoring tele-caller accounts and workloads.
Execution Role

Tele-caller Agent (TELE_CALLER)

Focused, distraction-free environment for daily calling targets, callback reminders, and questionnaire logging.

  • Personal Workload Dashboard: Today's pending calls, completed calls, and scheduled callbacks.
  • Live Call Console: Dialing assigned leads, duration recording, and questionnaire response entry.
  • Follow-up Management: Logging future callback dates and managing personal scheduled follow-ups.
  • Data Isolation: Zero access to other agents' leads, administrative tools, or global reports.
